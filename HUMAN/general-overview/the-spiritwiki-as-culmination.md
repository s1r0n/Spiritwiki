## The SpiritWiki as Culmination

Shows how the SpiritWik is the natural culmination of decades of exploring modern, scholarly controlled alternatives to the modern scholarly communication system with specific reference to my 1999 dissertation (Electronic Journals and the Transformation of Scholarly Publication).
