### What is the SpiritWiki?

The SpiritWiki is the living, semantically interlinked knowledge core of the Lightning Path—a Human Development Framework designed to accelerate insight, democratize expertise, and support planetary healing. Originating in 2007 as a personal digital notebook built on MediaWiki, it has evolved into a global public resource that replaces static, gatekept, and ideologically vulnerable textbooks and scholarly journals with a dynamic, CC0 public-domain Knowledge Ecosystem. Structured as a "Mycelium Web" of over 2,000 interconnected concepts curated by vetted Knowledge Stewards, it offers guided instruction, cognitive agency, safe exploration, and comprehensive integration of ancient wisdom and modern science, while remaining instantly updatable and AI-ready through its associated open-access Research Repository.
